**Status:** Draft — awaiting approval before any translation work starts.
**Brand rule (locked):** The brand name is always **Innerleaps** (lowercase `l`), in every language and context.

## 1. Decisions to make first

These choices steer the tone of the entire English site. Please pick one per row.

| # | Topic | Option A | Option B | My recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Spelling | US English (organization, color, program) | UK English (organisation, colour, programme) | **US** — broader international reach, matches "Program" in product names |
| 2 | Tone of voice / formality | Casual "you", direct, warm (matches NL "je") | Formal/corporate "you", more distant | **Casual** — preserves Innerleaps' approachable NL voice |
| 3 | "Innerlijke sprong" | Translate to **inner leap** (keeps the wordplay with the brand) | Keep Dutch "innerlijke sprong" as a brand concept | **Translate** — "inner leap" works as wordplay with Innerleaps |
| 4 | "Stressmanagement" program | **Stress Management** (clinical, widely searched) | **Stress Reduction** (outcome-focused, softer) | **Stress Management** — SEO + recognized term |
| 5 | "Ziekteverzuim" | **Absenteeism** (academic/HR term) | **Sick leave** (everyday term) | Use **both contextually**: "absenteeism" for analytical/HR copy, "sick leave" for everyday phrasing |
| 6 | "Werkgever / Werknemer" address style | "employer / employee" | "company / team member" | **employer / employee** — clearer for B2B audience |
| 7 | Currency in EN copy | Keep **€ EUR** everywhere | Show "€X (~$Y USD)" in marketing copy | **Keep € only** — we operate in NL, avoids stale FX rates |
